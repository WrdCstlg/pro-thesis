# P2 results

P2 ran ten arms, ten runs each, one world per run, with run seeds 1 to 10. The runs were made on
2026-10-23 by a binary built at `c22e678`, the commit that registered every arm's prediction. This file
scores those runs against the predictions in `../predictions/`, which stay exactly as registered. Verdict
and mechanism are scored separately. Every run is listed in `../runs/INDEX.md`, with a SHA-256 manifest of
its bundle in `../runs/manifests/`.

## What counts

- **100 runs, all complete.**
  - Every world realized its fault, `net.partition(role:leader)@3000..7500`, on the node the role probe
    named.
  - Every target world's recorded image read MATCH against its pinned digest (`tools/check-images.ps1`).
  - Every fixture world's boot logs named the intended build: `buggy/lease_ms=5000` for HC-01 and
    `kvfixed/lease_ms=300` for HC-02.
- **One run is excluded: HC-01 seed 1 (`r_2026_10_23_5eb3`).**
  - Its world booted on three volumes that its own compose project had left behind (OQ-087), and the
    arm's prediction excludes such a world.
  - The arm is scored on its other 9 runs. Whether to replace the run has not been decided.
- **No other world started on, or left behind, a volume of its own compose project.**

## Mechanism, arm by arm

All times are on the harness's virtual clock, whose zero is the start of DRIVE. `start_ms` is the realized
fault's recorded start.

- **HC-01.**
  - In all 9 counted runs, the witness read was served by the isolated node `kv-n2`, 1,281-2,322 ms after
    `start_ms`. That is inside the window the prediction registers: `start_ms` to the end of the 5 s lease.
  - The value it returned came from a write that `kv-n2` acknowledged about 205-271 ms *after* `start_ms`.
  - The registered falsifier says the stale value must come from a write before the partition. Read
    literally against the recorded start, that falsifier is met. Read against when the partition took
    effect (OQ-103), it is not.
  - The 9 of 9 FAIL rate (Wilson 95% interval 0.70-1.00) is above the registered range. The prior was
    46% per world, from multi-world runs that stopped at their first violation.
- **HC-02.**
  - The isolated leader answered reads invoked up to 232-284 ms after `start_ms`, inside its 300 ms lease,
    and none later.
  - Every world's boot logs read `lease_ms=300`.
- **ET-01, RQ-01.**
  - The isolated member answered reads with a value when they were invoked in the first 301-359 ms
    (etcd) or 298-335 ms (rqlite) after `start_ms`: 81-102 and 50-80 such reads per world. It answered
    none invoked later.
  - The registered falsifier ("a read served by the cut-off member inside the partition window that
    returns a value") is met literally inside that interval.
  - No `linearizable.kv` violation followed.
  - On rqlite, every `ok` write (85,211 over the 10 runs) was served by the node it was sent to.
- **CO-03.**
  - The isolated server answered reads invoked in the first 400 ms after `start_ms`.
  - The 1-6 reads per world invoked later were answered only after the heal, 5,263 ms or more after
    `start_ms`.
  - The verdict prediction fails only on the count: the 3 runs short of PASS are checker refusals (below),
    not rejections.
- **RQ-03, CO-02.**
  - Every witness read came before the partition: 81-314 ms into DRIVE (rqlite) and 63-327 ms into DRIVE
    (Consul).
  - Each was served by a node other than the one the partition later isolated.
  - Each prediction registered this case in advance: "replication lag produced the FAIL and the partition
    was not needed".
- **RE-01.**
  - In every world the driver issued all 60,000 operations within 4,940-5,457 ms of DRIVE starting, so
    none were issued after the heal at 7,500 ms.
  - Every operation went to `redis-n1`, and no reply was `-READONLY`.
  - The arm therefore never observed a period after a possible failover. Its 10 ESCAPEs say nothing
    about whether Sentinel discarded acknowledged writes (OQ-105).

## Calibration

These figures cover the 99 counted runs. INCONCLUSIVE runs are left out of each rate. Intervals are Wilson
score intervals at 95%.

| | Count | Rate | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRUE_REJECT / (TRUE_REJECT + ESCAPE), sensitivity | 29 / 53 | 0.547 | 0.415-0.673 |
| TRUE_PASS / (TRUE_PASS + FALSE_REJECTION), specificity | 36 / 36 | 1.000 | 0.904-1.000 |
| False-rejection rate | 0 / 36 | 0.000 | 0.000-0.096 |
| INCONCLUSIVE share of all counted runs | 10 / 99 | 0.101 | 0.056-0.176 |

- With no false rejection, the PPV of a FAIL is 1.000 at every base rate as a point value. What bounds it
  is the false-rejection rate's interval, up to 0.096.
- `thesis report` counts all 100 runs, because the tool does not know about the exclusion:
  - TRUE_PASS 36, TRUE_REJECT 30, ESCAPE 24, FALSE_REJECTION 0, INCONCLUSIVE 10;
  - sensitivity 0.5556, specificity 1.0000.
- The pooled sensitivity describes this arm mix, not the detector alone. 14 of the 24 ESCAPEs come from
  RQ-02 and CO-01, whose predictions expected mostly PASS against their FAIL pole. The other 10 come from
  RE-01, whose workload could not reach the window it targets.

## Caveats that apply to every arm

- **The recorded fault window starts early (OQ-103).**
  - The harness stamps a fault's `start_ms` when it begins the injection, not when the injection takes
    effect.
  - On four read paths, the isolated node kept serving for a few hundred milliseconds after `start_ms`.
    That fits an injection latency of roughly 230-360 ms, but the latency itself is not measured.
  - Any criterion that treats `start_ms` as the moment the partition holds reads that interval as service
    by an isolated node.
- **Fresh storage per world is not guaranteed (OQ-087).**
  - The operator checked every world's own compose project before and after it ran, and that check is
    what excluded HC-01 seed 1.
  - The harness itself does not yet check.
- **No independent re-derivation.** The violations above are the harness checker's. Re-deriving them from
  the raw histories by a path that does not read the verdict is not done, and remains open for the
  scorecard (`../scorecard.md`).

## What P2 does not show

- **Precision.** Ten runs per arm bound a rate only loosely; P3 (30 runs per arm, seeds 101-130) is the
  precision phase.
- **Redis failover loss.** RE-01 as registered cannot exercise it (OQ-105).
- **Whether the checker's budget, rather than the targets, decides some verdicts on rqlite and Consul**
  (OQ-104).
