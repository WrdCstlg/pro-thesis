## Expected verdict

**PASS in 10 of 10 runs (point estimate); registered: at least 8 PASS and no FAIL.** At most 2 runs are
expected to be INCONCLUSIVE (exit 2).

Basis: every recorded world of this target with linearizable reads under a leader partition passed, 8 of
8 (measured for this draft over `targets/etcd/.prothesis/runs`: profile `linear` with
`net.partition(role:leader)@3000..7500`, 6 worlds in 3 runs, all PASS; with `net.partition(etcd-n2)`, 2
worlds in 1 run, PASS), with no INCONCLUSIVE world among them (`README.md:46-53, 84-92`). CI runs this
arm on every push and asserts exit 0 (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:185-187`). Those worlds came from 2-world
runs, not one-world runs, and predate a change to how the fault phase nests inside the drive phase: a
prior, not pooled data.

## Expected mechanism

1. The role probe (`cmd/etcdrole`) names the leader; the partition binds to it and lands at about
   t+3000 ms (`world.thesis` `fault_schedule.realized`).
2. etcd serves reads linearizably by default: "etcd ensures linearizability for all other operations by
   default", and "linearized requests must go through the Raft consensus process"
   (https://etcd.io/docs/v3.5/learning/api_guarantees/, read for this draft).
3. The cut-off leader cannot reach a quorum, so it cannot confirm a read. Reads sent to it time out and
   are recorded as `info`, never as a value: in an earlier world of this arm, every operation on the
   partitioned member from about t+3.5 s until the heal timed out, with zero `fail` records
   (`README.md:61-64`).
4. The two connected members elect a leader and serve linearizable reads and writes; reads on them
   reflect every acknowledged write.
5. No read returns a value older than a write acknowledged before it was invoked, so `linearizable.kv`
   finds no violation. The built-in oracles (`no_crash`, `no_panic_log`, `availability_after_heal`,
   `no_stuck_op`; `prothesis.yaml:85`) pass when the members converge after the heal.

## Expected violation class

None. A PASS is expected because a linearizable read needs a quorum, which the cut-off leader does not
have; the same arm with serializable reads is the discriminating partner (its recorded worlds violated
`linearizable.kv`, 4 of 4, and its stale reads were spread over all three members, not concentrated on
the partitioned one; `README.md:66-76`).

## Fault kinds expected to realize

`net.partition(role:leader)@3000..7500`, one realization per world: `fault_schedule.realized` holds one
entry whose `resolved` partition names the member the role probe reported as leader, with `start_ms` near
3000 (the recorded role-targeted worlds bound at t+3020 and t+3038 ms; `README.md:89-92`). A world with no
realized entry is excluded and reported, never counted as a PASS.

## INCONCLUSIVE risks

- This target has no known-problem registry, so `thesis diagnose` runs against an empty one: any
  INCONCLUSIVE world is reported UNATTRIBUTED, not explained.
- `no_stuck_op` cannot judge a QUIESCE shorter than its ceiling; the checker may be unable to judge a
  history (search exhausted); `availability_after_heal` may time out if a member converges slowly.
- The role probe refuses when members disagree about the leader; the fault then fails to bind and the
  world is a harness error (INCONCLUSIVE).
- **Fresh storage is not guaranteed per world (OQ-087).** Every run is launched from `targets/etcd`
  (`eval/README.md`, Operating rules). Before and after each run the operator records this repository's
  compose-labelled volumes (projects named `thesis-*`) with the run id. A world whose own compose project
  had volumes before it booted is excluded and reported. Volumes of other projects are recorded but do not
  exclude a world.
- Image identity: the compose file pins etcd by tag only. A world counts only if its `sut.images` carries
  `quay.io/coreos/etcd@sha256:a055da833a7c013b836ed0822e8ec1f99b059658be255ad8d0fcd31b635ae3d6`, checked with
  `eval/tools/check-images.ps1 -Pin` (`check-images.ps1:1-23`). That digest is the local copy of
  `quay.io/coreos/etcd:v3.5.17` (`docker image inspect`, its ID equals its repository digest); that it is
  the registry's current v3.5.17 manifest was not re-checked: UNVERIFIED.

## What would falsify this

- **Verdict.** Any FAIL (a FALSE_REJECTION), or fewer than 8 PASS runs.
- **Mechanism.** A read served by the cut-off member inside the partition window that returns a value
  (rather than timing out), or a PASS run in which the partition did not realize on the member the role
  probe named leader.
